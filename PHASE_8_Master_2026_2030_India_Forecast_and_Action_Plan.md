### SECTION 5: MASTER PROFIT-BOOKING DISCIPLINE ("WHEN TO SELL VS. LET IT RIDE")

Selling a structural multi-bagger too early is the single most destructive mistake in wealth management. An investor who sold Titan in 2008 at a 30x P/E missed an ensuing 40x gain.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                 THE THREE PERMISSIBLE SELL TRIGGERS                              │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. [THESIS BREAKAGE]         Loss of competitive moat; gross margins contract >300 bps for 4 Qtrs│
│ 2. [FORENSIC FAILURE]        Promoter pledge breaches 15%; auditor resigns; opaque RPT loans     │
│ 3. [LUDICROUS VALUATION]     PEG ratio expands past 3.5x; price discounts 5+ years of perfection │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### 1. When to Sell a Holding Immediately:
*   **Forensic Breaches**: The moment a promoter pledges >15% of their equity to fund outside businesses, or an auditor resigns citing "discrepancies in accounts," **SELL 100% of the position with zero hesitation**.
*   **Terminal Business Model Disruption**: When technological substitution or regulatory bans permanently destroy pricing power (e.g., telecom telcos during the 2016 predatory pricing wars).
*   **Hyper-Euphoric Valuation Bubbles**: If a capital goods or defense stock with historical earnings growth of 18% reaches a trailing **P/E of 95x (PEG > 4.0x)**, trim **30% to 50% of the position** and re-allocate into undervalued Tier-1 large caps.

#### 2. When to Let It Ride for a Decade:
*   **RoCE > 20% and Reinvestment Runway**: As long as the business generates high returns on incremental capital and operates in a market where penetration is <20%, **NEVER SELL on optical P/E expansion alone**.
*   **Working Capital Efficiency**: Cash flow from operations matches or exceeds net profit year after year.
*   **Promoter Skin in the Game**: High promoter ownership (>50%) with continuous capacity expansion funded entirely through internal cash accruals.
